The 2026 KTM 990 RC R represents KTM’s long-awaited return to the street-legal supersport segment. After a decade-long hiatus since the RC8, the 990 RC R is designed to bridge the gap between a high-performance track weapon and a usable road bike, competing directly with the Ducati Panigale V2 and the Yamaha R9.

1. The Heart: Parallel-Twin Punch

The RC R uses a refined version of the 947cc LC8c parallel-twin found in the 990 Duke, but it has been "sharpened" for the racetrack.

Power: It produces 128 hp (94 kW) and 103 Nm of torque. The extra power comes from a more aggressive ECU map that allows the throttle bodies to open 100% (compared to 75% on the standard Duke).

Race-Shift Standard: In a nod to its track heritage, the gearbox can be reversed to a "GP shift" pattern (1 up, 5 down) as standard, without needing additional parts.

Aero-Driven Design: The bike features prominent winglets developed in KTM’s MotoGP wind tunnel, designed to provide downforce and stability under heavy braking and cornering.

2. High-Spec "R" Chassis

Unlike many "supersports" that use a rigid aluminum frame, KTM stuck with its signature steel trellis frame, but completely re-engineered it for the RC R.

Suspension Linkage: This is the first LC8c bike to feature a new rear linkage system for the WP APEX shock, significantly improving mechanical grip and comfort compared to the direct-link setup on the Duke.

WP APEX Pro Components: It comes with 48 mm fully adjustable WP APEX forks (stiffer and more focused) and a matching rear shock.

Brembo HyPure Brakes: It features the latest Brembo HyPure monobloc calipers—the same high-end equipment found on the latest Panigale V4—providing exceptional feel and stopping power.

3. The Digital Cockpit

The RC R debuts KTM's flagship electronic interface:

8.8-inch Touchscreen TFT: A massive, landscape-oriented screen that works with riding gloves. It features a split-screen mode for navigation and telemetry.

Advanced Electronics: It includes four standard modes (Rain, Street, Sport, Custom) plus an optional Track Mode that adds features like 5-level wheelie control, and launch control.

Sport ABS: Beyond standard cornering ABS, it offers Supermoto+ ABS, allowing the rider to slide the rear wheel while maintaining a safety net on the front.
